# Citation and Competition PRD

## Goal

Explain who AI recommends and which sources influence the answer.

## Scope

- Brand and competitor mention rate, rank and share of voice.
- Citation URL/domain normalization and source frequency.
- Prompt-, provider- and time-based comparisons.
- Raw-answer drill-down for every metric.

## Acceptance

Every chart value links to contributing responses. Users can identify prompts
where competitors appear but the brand does not, and see the cited sources.
